How to read this

X (blue) and O (orange) are the players. The three squares that are raised and glowing are the line being checked. A green line is a win. The strip at the front lists the 8 lines in the order the code checks them: R = row, C = column, D1 = diagonal from top left, D2 = diagonal from top right. ? means checking now, ✗ means not a win, ✓ means a win.
