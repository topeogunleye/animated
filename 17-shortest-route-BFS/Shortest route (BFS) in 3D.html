How to read this
  • Grey sphere: not discovered yet. Blue sphere with gold ring: discovered, waiting in the queue.
  • Raised gold sphere: the node being processed now. Green sphere: already taken from the queue.
  • Purple sphere and thick purple arrows: the final route. d=2 under a node: arrows from the start.
  • Arrows are one-way. Blue arrow: "we first reached this node from there" (parent map). Thick gold arrow: the arrow being evaluated now.
  • Rack in front: the queue, a plain list. head marks the next item to take. Faded blocks were processed without slow O(n) deletions.
