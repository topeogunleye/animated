The cache that forgets wisely, in 3D

Goal: remember results, and when full throw out the least recently used or oldest key.

drag to rotate · double-click to reset

Milestone 1/2 Ready to start guided audio tour.
How to read this

Back row: the calls in order. Grey = waiting, gold = active call, green = hit, orange = miss. Front row: the cache rack, slots left to right from OLDEST (thrown out first) to NEWEST. Each coloured block is a cached key; a raised block is the one just used or added. A red “evicted” label shows the key thrown out when full. Drag sideways to turn the view; double-click to reset.
