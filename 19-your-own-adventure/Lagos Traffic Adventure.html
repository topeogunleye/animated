Lagos Traffic Adventure: how a text game works

Goal: keep asking the player what to do, move them between rooms, and stop when they win, lose or type quit.

drag to rotate · double-click to reset

Tour
What the player sees
How to read this
  • Flat pad with a name = a room, a key in the rooms dictionary. The top of the screen is NORTH.
  • Blue pad = not visited yet. Green pad with #1, #2... = visited, in order.
  • Gold outline and the gold cone = the room the player is in now (the room variable).
  • Plain line = a door that works both ways. Line with an arrow = a one-way door.
  • Tall green block "ending: win" and tall red block "ending: lose" = the two endings.
  • Red box on a line = a locked door. It turns green and says "open" when the player holds the item it needs.
  • Gold coin = an item. The grey tray is the inventory list.
