How to read this

The back shelf is the original stock you pass in. It is locked: no function may change it. The front shelf is the new dictionary the function builds. Each bin is one item, each small crate is one unit, and the label shows the item name and quantity. A raised glowing bin is the one being read or changed. A gold beam shows an item being copied from the original to the new shelf. The big text in the middle is the value the function is working out or returns.
